What enrollment does — and does not do

This public static enrollment layer now issues a browser-local institutional identifier for each learner. K–12 learners receive a Student ID; self-directed adult learners receive a Scholar ID. Existing learners are backfilled non-destructively.

Until the protected Account Service is deployed, these identifiers are provisional local identifiers: they are stable within the Academy registry but are not yet globally reserved or authenticated across devices. The future server account service may confirm or migrate them without changing the learner-facing pathway.

No password, passcode, verification token, phone code, recovery secret, or credential is stored in this page, GitHub source, localStorage learner records, or family exports. Secure sign-in remains a server-side function.

NAIB may route a learner to the registered destination. It does not silently change formal placement. Archaemenes mentors after entry but does not independently award mastery or change placement.
