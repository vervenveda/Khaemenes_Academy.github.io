Front Door Protocol

The visible experience now follows the same architecture as the infrastructure underneath it.

Normal learner journey
Familycreates or resumes identity
Learnerone canonical learner ID
Placementexact grade or Higher Learning
NAIBinterprets and routes
Campuscorrect grade or scholar entry

Authority boundary: Family Registry owns learner identity and formal placement. NAIB receives, interprets, delegates, and connects. Archaemenes mentors but does not independently change grades, mastery, learner identity, or placement. Public pages remain learning and coordination surfaces, not secure authentication systems.
