What enrollment does — and does not do

This static public page creates browser-local Academy identities and placement records. It does not create a secure server account, verify custody, issue credentials, or expose protected infrastructure. Cross-device authenticated enrollment remains a future authorized-server function.

NAIB may route a learner to the registered destination. It does not silently change the learner's formal placement. Archaemenes mentors after entry but does not independently award mastery or change placement.
