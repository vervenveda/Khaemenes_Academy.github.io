Art 101 · Foundations of Visual Art

Art 101 is a proficiency-based creative studio course, not a ninth-grade placement. Families may explore it for an individual learner, and adult scholars may begin without entering the K–12 grade sequence.

Start with the artist portfolio and original studio evidence. Enrollment does not award mastery or unlock restricted progression. The Week 01 formative quiz is practice; official advancement requires separate Academy verification of at least 80% knowledge and studio work, approved evidence, and learner authorization.
