Integrated Learning

High school is more than a list of classes.

Khaemenes courses combine instruction, tools, projects, games, research, practice, reflection, and demonstrated mastery. The course remains authoritative for its own grades and progress.

LearnMeet the concept clearly.
ExploreManipulate, investigate, question.
WorkShow reasoning with real tools.
PracticeUse problems, games, and feedback.
ApplyProjects, labs, research, context.
MasterDemonstrate readiness to advance.
Readiness and mastery are different. Early course evidence helps NAIB recommend an appropriately challenging pathway. Once a pathway is selected, the course uses an 80% mastery standard for progression. Placement recommendations remain advisory; formal pathway decisions are handled through the appropriate family/Academy process rather than this student directory.

Student Experience

Built around the way students actually learn.

Your grade campus brings the pieces together so you can get to the work without navigating a maze of landing pages.

AP

Adaptive Core Pathways

Core subjects begin with readiness evidence and can offer foundation support, standard progression, or advanced challenge without turning placement into a label.

80

Mastery Progression

Courses use an 80% mastery standard so advancement is based on demonstrated understanding rather than simply reaching the end of a calendar week.

✎

Show Your Work

Handwriting, printables, graph paper, diagrams, coordinate work, calculations, notes, and digital creation give students more than one way to think.

β

Living Curriculum

Beta feedback and privacy-safe program signals help the Academy find unclear lessons, resource gaps, accessibility problems, and places where new tools or games could help.

A

Mentor Support

Archaemenes helps with planning, study direction, navigation, and practical next steps. NAIB supports readiness and learning-path recommendations where courses provide the evidence.

∞

Beyond the Course

Halls, archives, electives, research resources, creative studios, games, and Higher Learning pathways let curiosity continue beyond a required assignment.
