Four Connected Grade Years

One campus. Four distinct stages.

Each grade has a clear landing point, while department doors, research resources, and career preparation remain available across the full secondary sequence.

Grade 9 Learning Hall

Foundations, evidence, and academic fluency.

Pre-Algebra, Integrated Science 9, English 9, Social Studies 9, and the department entrances that support the first high-school year.

Grade 10 Learning Hall

Sophomore depth and stronger independence.

Mathematics 10, English 10, United States History Honors, science/health routes, and growing career awareness.

Grade 11 Learning Hall

Analysis, research, and future planning.

Junior-year writing, government/law/economics, mathematics planning, research tools, and deliberate exploration of postsecondary directions.

Grade 12 Learning Hall

Senior synthesis and transition.

English 12, senior social studies, mathematics planning, portfolio evidence, Career exploration, and a direct bridge into Higher Learning.

Adaptable Academic Pathways

Different levels without limiting potential.

Foundation, Core, Extended, and Capstone routes let learners work at an appropriate depth without losing access to the larger academic horizon.

01

Foundation

Clear instruction, smaller steps, repeated practice, visual support, and flexible pacing.

  • Prerequisite review
  • Guided examples
  • Accessible assessment
02

Core

A complete grade-level route balancing knowledge, communication, problem solving, and application.

  • Full course sequence
  • Projects and assessments
  • Portfolio-ready work
03

Extended

Research, advanced problems, interdisciplinary work, independent study, and deeper evidence use.

  • Enrichment and honors
  • Independent investigation
  • Advanced presentation
04

Capstone

Senior synthesis through projects, portfolios, research, service, presentations, and graduation records.

  • Thesis and portfolio
  • Public presentation
  • Graduation evidence
